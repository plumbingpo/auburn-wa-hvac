Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Auburn HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we plan service around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts and the heat fast. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Auburn HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Auburn and surrounding areas. Ongoing Protection

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Auburn HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, coil cleaning and condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand throughout the year. Each program is tailored to the property's specific equipment, usage patterns, and budget requirements.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
